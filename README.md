## About Me

I'm a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) student (expected 2027) with a strong theoretical foundation in **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, OCR, and Generative AI**. I build AI-assisted software end-to-end — designing workflows, integrating AI capabilities, and then doing the unglamorous but critical work: evaluating AI-generated output, debugging, auditing, testing, and refining until it's reliable.

I'm currently looking for opportunities in **AI/Generative AI, AI Evaluation, AI Quality Assurance, or Software Development**, where I can keep learning while contributing to AI systems people can actually trust.
